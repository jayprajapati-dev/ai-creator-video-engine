# 🎬 THE UNKNOWN CITY — ULTIMATE CREATIVE QUALITY DIRECTIVE
## Permanent Production Standard for All Future Episodes

> **MANDATE:** Every new episode of *THE UNKNOWN CITY (अनजान शहर)* must strictly adhere to this cinematic quality standard. It must feel like an award-winning animated short film, not a generic social media slideshow.

---

## 1. Creative Vision & Aesthetic
- **Atmosphere:** Psychological Thriller, Urban Legend, Cyber-Noir Mystery.
- **Palette (Sophisticated, Never Generic):**
  - Midnight Navy (`#05080F`, `#0A1424`)
  - Deep Charcoal (`#03060C`)
  - Cinematic Atmospheric Mist (`rgba(0, 212, 212, 0.15)`)
  - Muted Cyan Highlights (`#00D4D4`, `#0891B2`)
  - Warm Amber Indoor Light (`#F4A136`, `#D97706`)
  - Controlled Danger Red Accents (`#DC2626`, `#991B1B`)
- **Visual Depth:** Every scene must have at least 3 layers:
  1. *Foreground:* Props, bars, silhouettes, blur elements.
  2. *Midground:* Character (Aarav), primary story action.
  3. *Background:* Detailed stylized environments, atmospheric perspective, lighting gradients.

---

## 2. Character Animation Standard (Aarav)
- **Fluid Micro-Movements:** Continuous breathing cycles, realistic eye blink intervals (every 70–90 frames), subtle eye-darting (fear response).
- **8+ Dynamic Rigged Poses:** `standing`, `walking`, `running`, `lookAround`, `fear`, `shock`, `phone`, `turning`.
- **Acting Principles:**
  - Anticipation (crouch before jump/run).
  - Reaction shots (quick facial close-up when a scary reveal happens).
  - Physical interaction (phone light illuminating his face, trembling limbs).
- **Zero Idle Stagnation:** Never repeat a static pose for more than 45 frames (1.5s) without camera move or character breath/reaction.

---

## 3. Environment & Environmental Storytelling
- Real, recognizable stylized Indian urban spaces:
  - Empty Metro Stations, Locked Shopping Malls, Dark Basements (B1/B2), Endless Corridor Mazes, Abandoned Control Rooms, Stairwells.
- Details that tell the story:
  - Flickering fluorescent tube with audio buzz.
  - Reflections on polished floors or CCTV glass.
  - Surveillance camera tracking the character.
  - Signboards with cryptic Hindi/English text.

---

## 4. Cinematic Camera & Editing Rhythm
- **Shot Variety:**
  - Extreme Wide (Establishing scene, isolation).
  - Medium Shot (Character navigating the space).
  - Close-Up / Reaction (Facial dread, phone screen, elevator indicator).
  - Dutch Angle / CCTV Low-Angle (Unsettling tension).
- **Rhythm (Every 3-4 Seconds):**
  - Fast opening hook (0–4s): Immediate mystery event.
  - Build-up (4–25s): Gradual dread, clues.
  - Suspense peak (25–45s): Sudden unexplainable event.
  - Twist reveal (45–54s): Pattern interruption.
  - Cliffhanger & Audience Vote (54–60s): High-tension choice (Option A vs B).

---

## 5. 6-Layer Professional Sound Design
- **Layer 1:** Ambient Room Tone / Stereo Drone (Mall, Metro, Wind, Basement Hum).
- **Layer 2:** Character Foley (Footsteps on tiles, fabric rustle, heavy breathing).
- **Layer 3:** Mechanical Environmental SFX (Elevator motor, shutter slam, light switch click, door creak).
- **Layer 4:** Suspense Tension (Sub-bass riser, heartbeat thump, dissonant strings).
- **Layer 5:** Edge TTS Narration (`hi-IN-MadhurNeural`, crystal clear, balanced at 1.0 volume).
- **Layer 6:** Cinematic Impact Hits (Jump cut stabs, bass drops on reveals).

---

## 6. Typography & Motion Graphics
- **Devanagari First:** Large, readable, clean system fonts (`Noto Sans Devanagari`, `Mangal`).
- **Kinetic Highlighting:** 1–4 words per fragment, with high-impact words highlighted in Red (`#DC2626`), Cyan (`#00D4D4`), or Gold (`#F59E0B`).
- **UI Overlays:** Minimalist timestamp, REC dot, CCTV camera tag (e.g. `CAM 07`), subtle corner crop marks. Never clutter the focal point.

---

## 7. Interactive Cliffhanger Protocol
- End every episode with an impossible dilemma:
  - Two high-stakes paths (e.g., *A: Basment B1 me jao* vs *B: Security Room me chhupo*).
  - Pulsing timer countdown (6s).
  - Direct prompt for comments to drive algorithm watch-time and virality.
